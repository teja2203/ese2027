D — Nocturne ★ recommended

Cinematic dark, pro-tool precision

Layered deep blacks, hairline borders, indigo-violet signal, gradient numerals, glassy blur nav. Linear/Arc/Verne-grade. Celebrations: ripple, aurora sweep, bloom, engraved bottom-sheet.

Differs: cinematic depth · glass + blur · gradient display type · aurora motion
