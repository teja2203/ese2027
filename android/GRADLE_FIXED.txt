## Why It Failed

Gradle 9.6.1 removed the internal `HasConvention` API. AGP 8.2.0 still used it. Downgrading to Gradle 8.2 with updated AGP 8.2.2 resolves the issue.

3. **Gradle Sync** will complete successfully
4. **Build → Make Project** should succeed
